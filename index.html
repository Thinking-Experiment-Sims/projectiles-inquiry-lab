💡 Pedagogical Core: Because gravity accelerates both the banana and monkey downwards at the identical rate ($a_y = -g$), both drop below the line of sight by exactly $\Delta y = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$. Aiming directly at the monkey ensures a hit at any launch speed that reaches before ground impact!
