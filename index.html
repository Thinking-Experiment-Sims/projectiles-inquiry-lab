💡 What You Can Do in This Lab

🐵 1. Feed the Monkey (Gizmo Activity)

Question: Where should you aim a banana to feed a falling monkey that releases its branch the instant the cannon fires?

  1. Turn on Line of Sight or click Auto-Aim to point directly at the monkey.
  2. Launch the Banana at v0 = 20 m/s, then 30 m/s, then 45 m/s.
  3. Inspect the synchronized Strobes: both drop by the exact same distance Δy = ½gt² from the line of sight!
  4. Toggle Zero-G Ghost to see the straight-line path without gravity.
🎯 2. Mark Rober's Automated Dartboard

Question: How does Mark's motorized target guarantee a bullseye, and will the dart clear the workshop ceiling?

  1. Click Load Worksheet Values (v0 = 15.0 m/s, α = 40°, x = 5.0 m).
  2. Watch the motorized carriage slide along the track to catch the bullseye at height H = 5.05 m.
  3. Review Question 2: The dart apex reaches hmax = 6.45 m. Lower the ceiling slider below 6.45 m to test a ceiling collision!
📋 3. Classroom Notes (Cliff & 70m Building)

Question: A projectile is fired from a 320 m cliff at 42 m/s (30°). Does it clear the 70 m obstacle building?

  1. Inspect the Full Solution and Cornell T-Chart: horizontal velocity is constant (vx = 36.4 m/s) while vertical motion accelerates downward (ay = -10 m/s²).
  2. Find flight time to the far wall (x = 310 m): t = 8.52 s.
  3. Verify height: y = 135.8 m > 70 m (clears roof by 65.8 m!).
📝 4. Classic 2D Trajectory Challenges

Structured Inquiry Scenarios:

  1. Box Roll-Off: Ball rolls off 2 m box at 5 m/s. Range = 3.19 m (t = 0.64 s).
  2. 100m Cliff Launch: Lands 300 m away. Solve for launch speed: v0 = 66.4 m/s.
  3. Soccer Player Kick: Given vx = 20 m/s, vy = 12 m/s. Solve for v0 = 23.3 m/s, θ = 31.0°, range = 49.0 m.
  4. Tennis Serve Challenge: Serves horizontally at 40 m/s from 2.5 m. Clears 0.92 m net at 12 m (y = 2.06 m), but lands at 28.6 m > 18.4 m (Invalid / Long!).
